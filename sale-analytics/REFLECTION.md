# Ethical & Technical Reflection

1. **Data Privacy Act of 2012:** Before analyzing real order data containing customer personal information, the seller must obtain explicit consent from data subjects, register the data processing system with the National Privacy Commission (NPC), and implement strict security measures. We would remove or mask columns such as customer names, exact delivery addresses, and phone numbers to ensure anonymization.
2. **COD Cancellation Impact:** Stopping Cash on Delivery (COD) based solely on high cancellation rates would hurt unbanked customers, students, and regional buyers who lack credit cards or e-wallets like GCash or Maya. The seller should weigh the financial cost of logistics returns against the potential loss of market reach and customer acquisition.
3. **Big Data Vs:** 
   - *Volume:* Appears in the 1,000,000 simulated orders processed during the lab.
   - *Velocity:* Appears in the hourly traffic spikes during midnight and evening rush hours on November 11.
4. **Internet of Things (IoT):** Integrating IoT GPS trackers on delivery vehicles could add real-time transit telemetry data to this analysis, improving estimated delivery time accuracy. However, a major drawback is the high cost of hardware deployment and maintenance across logistics fleets.
